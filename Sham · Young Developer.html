04 · How it works

How it works. It's just three steps.

Step 1The buyer pays

The money goes into Escrowly and stays there. The seller can see it's paid.

Step 2The seller delivers

They send the item or do the work, then mark it as delivered.

Step 3The money is released

The buyer confirms and the seller gets paid. If there's a problem, there's a dispute option.
